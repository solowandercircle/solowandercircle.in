QUESTIONS

Frequently asked.

Who can join SoloWanderCircle?+

Anyone who enjoys travelling, meeting new people and exploring respectfully can join.

Do I have to be from Hyderabad?+

No. Hyderabad is one of our community hubs, but travellers from anywhere can join suitable trips and meetups.

Are the meetups free?+

Our community meetups are generally free. You only pay for anything you personally order or for a paid activity clearly mentioned.

Are trips paid?+

Trip costs vary by destination and itinerary. Each trip will clearly mention what is included.

Can I join a trip alone?+

Absolutely. Many people join without knowing anyone beforehand. That's the whole idea.
